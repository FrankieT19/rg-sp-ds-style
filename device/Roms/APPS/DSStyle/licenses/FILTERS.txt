Filter credits
==============

LCD grid: inspired by lcd1x, by Gigaherz and jdgleaver.
https://github.com/libretro/slang-shaders/blob/master/handheld/shaders/lcd1x.slang

Pixel Transparency: adapted from Pixel Transparency v2.2 by Matt Akins
(mattakins), copyright 2025-2026.
https://github.com/libretro/slang-shaders/tree/master/handheld/shaders/pixel_transparency

The effects run in the launcher. Pixel Transparency uses a fixed backing,
reduced colour cast and an unblurred shadow with a one-pixel offset.
No RetroArch shader settings are changed.
